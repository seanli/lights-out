# learn

The Learn screen (`screen:learn`): "Basics" (five classic lessons) and "Board guides" (one per room in `levels.packs` with copy in `learn.guides`). At most five short steps each, one idea per step. Owns the `learn` slice.

- `learn:open {lessonId?}` → lesson, guide (`guide-<room id>`) or the list, step 0, `learn:lesson_opened`, `nav:go learn`. Unknown id (or no rooms yet) → `learn:refused`. Opened from another screen, Back and Done return there (`nav:back`).
- `learn:step {delta}` → Previous/Next; past the last step completes it (`learn:lesson_completed`, saved under `rules.json → learn.saveKey`).
- Practice: `learn:new_practice` / `learn:press {index}` on a mini board of the lesson's shape (cols × rows, gaps drawn empty, never cells); `learn:practice_solved`. Never touches stars. Slots under `--aim-below` (40px, as on the main board) aim with `src/lib/aim.ts` ('aim': hold, slide, lift; the tip shows only after a miss or a drag); the Tower's board scrolls inside its box ('tap': a swipe never presses), opens on its lights, and Assist scrolls to its ring. Lesson 1 says "tap it again to undo it" after a wrong tap; lesson 2's boards are 2–3 shapes that never touch; lesson 5's Assist rings a shortest answer.
- Layout: the list is `.section > .menu-list > .menu-row` (picture, title, one dim line, done check or empty ring). A lesson is `.screen-header` (full rectangles: "6×6 · Step 1 of 4"), dots, two lines of text, the stage, `.bar` (Previous / Next). The stage's fit box (a size container) holds the picture as large as it fits: a board never taller than at full width, so it centres with its line and buttons; 2 figures stack and 3 go two over one on a tall stage; a scrolling list fades at an edge with more. "Go deeper" sits under the last idea before practice.
- Lesson 4 teaches: chase, tap the top lights for your row, chase again; "Go deeper" (`echo-more`, `shortest-more`) holds the theory and the drill (`drill.ts`).
- Guides (`guides.ts`) pick their steps from the maths: chase clears all (none today); full rectangles (5×6, 6×6, 7×7, 8×8, 10×10, Tower): chase → one bottom light (a letter over each column picks it; its top lights are outlined, then played) → several (a light tapped twice says it cancels) → practice; shaped rooms (Cross, Ring, Diamond): tap shapes → spot → chase (stuck) → the top-edge fix (every top-edge light outlined) → practice. Rectangle demos carry the column letters. Guide practice has a coach line by phase (`coachPhase`: chase; "Bottom light C: tap A and E, then chase again."; chase again); a rectangle's board is a few low taps (a short first chase) plus 1–2 lone bottom lights. Example boards come from fixed-order searches on the real shape; long demos speed up to about `demoMaxMs`.
- Maths (`method.ts`) comes from the solver: the classic table, quiet patterns, echoes, per-light top taps (`rectTopSets`) and the rule they follow (`rectRule`: straight above, three above, the 6×6 and 10×10 two-aside rules, or none), and for any shape the chase, start/end cells and `createFixer`. Tests prove each claim on every room, and that each guide's words name its rule.
- Demos: the line reads "Top row: tap 1 of 17"; one that stops with lights on says how many are left (`demoEnd`); a tap on the board pauses it, then steps. Marks: "tap here" is the tutorial's dashed pointer on practice; demos and pictures dot the next tap and outline what to tap; teal stays the paid Hint's.
- Patterns panel (`reference.ts` → `slot:reference`, never marks the main board), one structure on every board: the status with the guide link (`[data-action="guide"]`; classic: all lessons) on line 1, the rule (`[data-reference-rule]`: classic the matched echo, rectangles `rectRule`; whole or hidden), then your bottom row (`[data-reference-bottom]`) over the top taps (empty until bottom-row time), shaped rooms' picture with the fix ringed. Only what is relevant now, on every board: the 7-row table lives in lesson 4.
- Every sentence is in `copy.json → learn`; styles in `learn.css` (tokens only). Services: `view`, `solver`, `rng`; optional `storage`, `clock`. Reads `levels` (rooms) and `progress` (tile glyphs). Console: `learn.open [id]`, `learn.table`.
