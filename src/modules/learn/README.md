# learn

The Learn screen (`screen:learn`): "Basics" (five classic lessons) and "Board guides" (one per room in `levels.packs` with copy in `learn.guides`). At most five short steps each, one idea per step. Owns the `learn` slice.

- `learn:open {lessonId?}` → lesson, guide (`guide-<room id>`) or the list, step 0, `learn:lesson_opened`, `nav:go learn`. Unknown id (or no rooms yet) → `learn:refused`. Opened from another screen, Back and Done return there (`nav:back`).
- `learn:step {delta}` → Previous/Next; past the last step completes it (`learn:lesson_completed`, saved under `rules.json → learn.saveKey`).
- Practice: `learn:new_practice` / `learn:press {index}` on a mini board of the lesson's shape; `learn:practice_solved`. Never touches stars. A stuck switch only shakes (a press on it is refused, no tap counted). Slots under `--aim-below` aim with `src/lib/aim.ts`; the Tower's practice (and the cube's net on a short phone) scrolls in its box, presses on release, opens on its lights; its demos show the whole board (18px lights at least). Lesson 1 says "tap it again to undo it"; lesson 2's boards are 2–3 shapes that never touch; lesson 5 teaches the Restart move (chase to find your row, Restart, the fix first, one chase: par), and its practice has Restart (`learn:restart_practice`) and an Assist that rings a shortest answer. Shaped and twist guides practise on boards made like the levels (`Lesson.tangled`: `lib/puzzle.ts makePuzzle`, symmetric where the board allows, else tangled).
- Mini boards draw every grid as the play board does: gaps keep their slot; a honeycomb places each light on two half-columns (odd rows half a light right); the cube is its net, six face panels with a seam; wired switches show their circuit traces and stuck ones their plate (`lib/icons.ts`). A honeycomb tile's face is the clipped hexagon; its outline and Assist's ring are an unclipped hexagon around it.
- Layout: the list is `.section > .menu-list > .menu-row`. A lesson is `.screen-header` (full rectangles: "7×7 · Step 1 of 4"), dots, two lines of text, the stage (a fit box holding the picture as large as it fits, then the line and buttons), `.bar`.
- Guides (`guides.ts`) pick their steps from the room and teach what works on tangled levels (no whole shape to spot): full rectangles (7×7, 8×8, the 8×16 Tower): chase → one bottom light (the top row over the bottom row as strips; a bottom light picks) → several → practice; shaped rooms (Ring, Diamond): tap shapes → chase → the top-edge fix → practice. Twist rooms by pack kind: Hex (flower of seven / overlaps cancel / two side by side), Cube (a plus on the net / a front corner's plus on three faces / two side by side), Wired (three switches / a light's switches outlined / work back), Stuck (a plate's light flips / chase through it / a light over a plate: the top-edge fix). Twist practice: Assist rings a shortest answer in reading order (`spotOrder`); Stuck uses chase → fix → chase. Example boards come from fixed-order searches on the real shape.
- Maths (`method.ts`) comes from the solver: the classic table (each bottom row's fix with the fewest taps in all, chase included), quiet patterns, echoes, per-light top taps (`rectTopSets`: one chase per top light and GF(2), so a 20-wide custom board is instant; `tapsFor` any row a chase can leave) and their rule (`rectRule`), and for any square grid (gaps, stuck switches) the chase, start/end cells and `createFixer`. Tests prove each claim on every room.
- Demos: "Top row: tap 1 of 17"; one that stops with lights on says how many are left; a tap on the board pauses it, then steps. Marks: practice "tap here" is the dashed pointer; demos dot the next tap and outline what matters (top lights, top-edge lights, a wired light's switches); teal stays the paid Hint's.
- Patterns panel (`reference.ts` → `slot:reference`, never marks the main board): line 1 the status with the guide link (`[data-action="guide"]`; classic: all lessons), the rule (`[data-reference-rule]`), then the live strips. Classic: your row + its echo; rectangles: `rectRule` + bottom row over the top taps (wider than tall: chased sideways, the right column over its numbered left-column taps); shaped rooms and stuck switches: the picture with the fix ringed. Honeycombs, cubes and wired boards (`[data-reference-kind="hex|cube|wired"]`, custom ones too, and stuck boards too wide for the fix table): the room's one-line tip (`learn.reference.tips`) and that room's guide.
- Every sentence is in `copy.json → learn`; styles in `learn.css` (tokens only). Services: `view`, `solver`, `rng`; optional `storage`, `clock`. Reads `levels` (rooms) and `progress` (tile glyphs). Console: `learn.open [id]`, `learn.table`.
