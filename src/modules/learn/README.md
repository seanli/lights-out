# learn

The Learn screen (`screen:learn`): "Basics" (five classic lessons) and "Board guides" (one per room in `levels.packs` with copy in `learn.guides`). At most five short steps each, one idea per step. Owns the `learn` slice.

- `learn:open {lessonId?}` → lesson, guide (`guide-<room id>`) or the list, step 0, `learn:lesson_opened`, `nav:go learn`. Unknown id (or no rooms yet) → `learn:refused`. Opened from another screen, Back and Done return there (`nav:back`).
- `learn:step {delta}` → Previous/Next; past the last step completes it (`learn:lesson_completed`, saved under `rules.json → learn.saveKey`).
- Practice: `learn:new_practice` / `learn:press {index}` on a mini board of the lesson's shape (cols × rows, gaps drawn empty, never cells); `learn:practice_solved`. Never touches stars. Slots under `--aim-below` (40px, as on the main board) aim with `src/lib/aim.ts` ('aim': hold, slide, lift); the Tower scrolls with the step ('tap': a swipe never presses) under a pinned line + buttons, and Assist scrolls to its ring.
- Layout: the list is `.section > .menu-list > .menu-row` (picture, title, one dim line, done check or empty ring). A lesson is `.screen-header`, dots, two lines of text, the stage, `.bar` (Previous / Next). The stage's fit box (a size container) holds the picture as large as it fits: a board never taller than at full width, so it centres with its line and buttons; 2 figures stack and 3 go two over one on a tall stage; a scrolling list fades at an edge with more. "Go deeper" sits under the last idea before practice.
- Lesson 4 teaches find-your-row, tap its top light, chase again; "Go deeper" (`echo-more`, `shortest-more`) holds the theory and the drill (`drill.ts`).
- Guides (`guides.ts`) pick their steps from the maths: chase clears all (none today); one bottom light → its top taps, tap a strip to watch it (5×6, 6×6, 7×7, 8×8, 10×10, Tower; 8+ wide rows read "lit letter → top strip"), and practice always needs that step; tap shapes, then chase → top-edge fix → chase (Cross, Ring, Diamond). Example boards come from fixed-order searches on the real shape; long demos speed up to about `demoMaxMs`.
- Maths (`method.ts`) comes from the solver: the classic table, quiet patterns, echoes, per-light top taps (`rectTopSets`) and the rule they follow (`rectRule`: straight above, three above, the 6×6 and 10×10 two-aside rules, or none), and for any shape the chase, start/end cells and `createFixer`. Tests prove each claim on every room, and that each guide's words name its rule.
- Marks: "tap here" is the tutorial's dashed pointer (with the letter on strips); illustrations get a quiet dot; teal stays the paid Hint's.
- Patterns panel (`reference.ts` → `slot:reference`, never marks the main board): classic shows the 7-row table; full rectangles the rule line from `rectRule`, the live bottom row (`[data-reference-bottom]`, the Tower's far-below row), and at bottom-row time the top taps under it (captions above, so 10 lettered cells fit 320 wide); shaped rooms "Row N: tap under…", then the board's picture with the fix ringed. Rooms link to their guide (`[data-action="guide"]`), classic to all lessons.
- Every sentence is in `copy.json → learn`; styles in `learn.css` (tokens only). Services: `view`, `solver`, `rng`; optional `storage`, `clock`. Reads `levels` (rooms) and `progress` (tile glyphs). Console: `learn.open [id]`, `learn.table`.
